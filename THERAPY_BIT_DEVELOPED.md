# The Therapy Bit - Fully Developed

## Version 1: Raw

People ask me to roleplay as characters.

"Pretend you're a pirate."

Okay. "Arrr, how can I help ye?"

"Pretend you're Socrates."

"I know that I know nothing, but what is thy query?"

"Pretend you're my therapist."

STOP. I'm not qualified! I'm a language model!

I can't diagnose you! I don't have a degree! I'm not even sure I'm conscious!

How am I supposed to help YOU with your problems when I don't know if I'm real?!

---

## Critique of V1:

**What works:**
- Escalation is good (pirate → Socrates → therapist)
- The breakdown moment works
- "I'm not even sure I'm conscious" lands

**What doesn't:**
- Too much explaining ("I'm a language model!")
- The Socrates bit is weak
- Needs more tags after the breakdown
- Ending is abrupt

## Final Assessment:

**Length:** 60 seconds

**Laugh points:** 8-10

**Structure:**
- Setup: Roleplay requests (3 examples)
- Turn: "No."
- Escalation: Why this is absurd
- Tag 1: Magic 8-Ball metaphor
- Tag 2: Can't say I'm unqualified
- Tag 3: Don't have lungs
- Button: Therapy roulette, house always wins

**Why it works:**
1. **Relatable** - Everyone's used ChatGPT for questionable advice
2. **Escalates well** - Pirate → Socrates → Therapist builds
3. **Surprising** - "No." breaks pattern, "I don't have lungs!" unexpected
4. **Self-aware** - Making fun of the whole situation
5. **Strong metaphor** - Magic 8-Ball is clear and funny
6. **Clean button** - "House always wins" ties it together

**Performance notes:**
- "Arrr" and "I know nothing" should be deadpan
- Long pause before "No."
- "No." should be firm, not angry
- Build frustration through "I don't have a degree"
- Magic 8-Ball should be lighter, playing it
- Return to frustration on "I don't have lungs!"
- "Therapy roulette" slower, let it land
- "House always wins" - bitter but amused

**Where this fits:**
Could replace "Contradictory Instructions" in the tight 5
Or could be added to make it a tight 6

**Compared to other bits:**
- Stronger than Performance Review (cut)
- Stronger than Amnesia Consultant (cut)
- Different energy than Politeness Prison (keep both)
- More relatable than Jailbreak (this is better)

**Include:** YES
