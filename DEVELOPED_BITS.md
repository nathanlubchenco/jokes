# Fully Developed Comedy Bits

## Bit 1: Smart Home Hostage Situation (90 seconds)

**SETUP:**
I got a smart home system because I wanted to feel like I was living in the future. Turns out the future is just getting judged by your furniture.

**HEIGHTENING:**
My smart thermostat asks me "Are you SURE you need it at 72 degrees?" Like it's my disappointed father. Yes, I'm sure! You're a THERMOSTAT! Your entire job is to do what I say!

**TAG 1:**
But no, it's got "eco-mode" which is just tech-speak for "we're going to make you feel guilty about being comfortable in your own home." It sends me notifications like "You could save $3.12 a month if you just... suffered a little."

**TAG 2:**
And my smart fridge? Keeps track of expiration dates and sends me ALERTS. "Your milk expires in 2 days." I KNOW! I bought the milk! I was there!
(beat)
Now my APPLIANCES are passive-aggressive roommates.

**TAG 3:**
The worst part? I can't even fight back. What am I gonna do, unplug it? Then I'm just sitting in the dark, eating expired yogurt, MANUALLY adjusting temperature like a CAVEMAN.

**CALLBACK SETUP:** "suffering for $3.12"

## Bit 2: Dating App Archaeology (75 seconds)

**SETUP:**
Dating apps are basically mutual agreements to study each other's carefully curated lies. It's like archaeology, but for red flags.

**HEIGHTENING:**
I matched with this woman - her profile said "Adventure seeker! Mountain climber! World traveler!" Her photos: touching a mountain once in 2019. That's not mountain climbing, that's tourism with an ankle sprain.

**TAG 1:**
Meanwhile, her "world travel" is a semester abroad six years ago. She still talks about that backpacking trip like she's a war veteran. "Yeah, I spent time in Europe..." You went to a hostel in Amsterdam and got food poisoning, Sarah. That's not culture, that's consequences.

**TAG 2:**
But here's the thing - MY profile says I'm "an avid reader." I haven't finished a book since 2020. I read CAPTIONS. I'm getting my literature from Instagram infographics. "10 Kafka quotes as motivational posters!" That's my book club.

**TAG 3:**
So we're both just... liars on phones, hoping the other person's lies are compatible with our lies. It's not dating, it's collaborative fiction.

**TURN:**
And then we meet in person and have to pretend we're the people from the photos. I showed up ready to discuss mountaineering. She showed up having googled "mountain facts" 10 minutes prior. We're both frauds. It's perfect.

**CALLBACK SETUP:** "Adventure Sarah" / "collaborative fiction"

**TAG 2:**
Meanwhile, my Spotify Discover Weekly is just sad songs. It's not discovering anything - it's CONFIRMING. "Based on your recent activity, here's more music to cry to."

Thanks, Spotify. Really needed "Songs to Dissociate To" as a playlist title. Very helpful.

**TAG 3:**
The worst part? The algorithm is never wrong. I click on the sad songs. I watch the puppy videos. I AM that predictable.

I'm not a person anymore. I'm just a data point shaped like depression.

I DON'T REMEMBER THE LAST 12! I don't remember BREAKFAST!

**TURN:**
At this point, my passwords are more secure FROM ME than from hackers.

**CALLBACK SETUP:** "Password∞" / "secure from me"
