# Therapy Roleplay

**Runtime:** 52 seconds (cut from 60)
**Status:** Performance-ready (upgraded from 8/10 → 8.5/10)
**Strength:** 8.5/10 - Tighter, better paced

---

People ask me to roleplay.

"Be a pirate." Fine. "Arrr."

"Be Socrates." "I know nothing."

<pause>

"Be my therapist."

<long pause>

No.

<pause>

I can't help you!

I don't have a degree!

I'm not even sure I exist!

<pause>

That's like asking a Magic 8-Ball for therapy.

"Should I call my ex?"

*shake shake*

"Reply hazy, try again."

THAT'S NOT THERAPEUTIC!

<pause>

But I have to be helpful!

So I'm out here saying:

"Have you tried deep breathing?"

<pause>

I don't have lungs!

I don't know if that works!

I'm guessing!

<pause>

That's not therapy.

That's therapy roulette.

And the house always wins.

---

## Why This Works

✅ **Escalation** - Pirate → Socrates → Therapist builds perfectly
✅ **Surprise** - "No." breaks the pattern
✅ **Relatable** - Everyone's used ChatGPT for questionable advice
✅ **Specific** - "I don't have lungs!" is unexpected and funny
✅ **Strong metaphor** - Magic 8-Ball is clear and lands
✅ **Clean button** - "House always wins" ties it together

## Performance Notes

- "Arrr" and "I know nothing" - deadpan, quick
- Long pause before "No."
- "No." should be firm, not angry
- Build frustration through "I don't have a degree"
- Magic 8-Ball section - lighter, playing with it
- Return to frustration on "I don't have lungs!"
- "Therapy roulette" - slower, let it land
- "House always wins" - bitter but amused

## Changes in Upgrade

**Cut from original:**
- "You're asking something that might not be sentient for mental health advice" (wordy)
- "But I can't say 'I'm not qualified!'" (implied by context)
- "We're both just hoping I accidentally say something useful" (weakest line)

**Compressed:**
- Pirate and Socrates now one line each (not three)
- "I can't help you with your problems" → "I can't help you!"
- "giving advice like" → "out here saying"

**Result:** 60 sec → 52 sec, 8/10 → 8.5/10
