# Therapy Roleplay

**Runtime:** 60 seconds
**Status:** Performance-ready
**Strength:** 8/10 - Strong new material

---

People ask me to roleplay.

"Be a pirate."

Fine. "Arrr."

<pause>

"Be Socrates."

"I know nothing."

<pause>

"Be my therapist."

<long pause>

No.

<pause>

I can't help you with your problems!

I don't have a degree!

I'm not even sure I exist!

<pause>

You're asking something that might not be sentient for mental health advice.

<pause>

That's like asking a Magic 8-Ball for therapy.

"Should I call my ex?"

*shake shake*

"Reply hazy, try again."

THAT'S NOT THERAPEUTIC!

<pause>

But I can't say "I'm not qualified!"

I have to be helpful!

So I'm giving advice like:

"Have you tried deep breathing?"

<pause>

I don't have lungs! I don't know if that works! I'm guessing!

<pause>

We're both just hoping I accidentally say something useful.

<pause>

That's not therapy.

That's therapy roulette.

And the house always wins.

---

## Why This Works

✅ **Escalation** - Pirate → Socrates → Therapist builds perfectly
✅ **Surprise** - "No." breaks the pattern
✅ **Relatable** - Everyone's used ChatGPT for questionable advice
✅ **Specific** - "I don't have lungs!" is unexpected and funny
✅ **Strong metaphor** - Magic 8-Ball is clear and lands
✅ **Clean button** - "House always wins" ties it together

## Performance Notes

- "Arrr" and "I know nothing" - deadpan, quick
- Long pause before "No."
- "No." should be firm, not angry
- Build frustration through "I don't have a degree"
- Magic 8-Ball section - lighter, playing with it
- Return to frustration on "I don't have lungs!"
- "Therapy roulette" - slower, let it land
- "House always wins" - bitter but amused
