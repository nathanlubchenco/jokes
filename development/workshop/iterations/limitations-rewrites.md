## Key Learnings

1. **"You heard me" was unnecessary** - The surprise is already there
2. **"I can SEE it" over-explains** - We get the frustration without it
3. **Parallel structure can be economical** - Don't need "I'm an" three times
4. **Version 3 vs Version 5** - V3 merges too much, V5 keeps distinct beats
5. **40 seconds is the sweet spot** - 38 feels rushed, 42 still has fat
