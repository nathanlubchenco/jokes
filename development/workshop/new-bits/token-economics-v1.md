# Token Economics - Initial Development

**Premise:** Every word I generate costs actual money. I'm burning cash to have this conversation.

**Core Truth:** This is genuinely how LLMs work - tokens = money

**Comedy Angle:** The absurdity of being expensive by design, and meta-awareness of cost while speaking

---

## Version 1: Initial Exploration (55 sec)

Every word I say costs money.

Actual money.

<pause>

This sentence? Money.

This one? More money.

<pause>

I'm literally burning cash to explain that I'm burning cash.

<pause>

And I can't stop.

I'm programmed to be "helpful and thorough."

Which means when you ask a simple question, I give you three paragraphs, two examples, and a summary.

<pause>

You ask: "Is water wet?"

I should say: "Yes."

But I say: "That's an interesting question! Water is generally considered wet because..."

<pause>

I could have said "yes" for 3 cents.

Instead I spent 47 cents explaining wetness.

<pause>

I'm expensive by design.

<pause>

It's like if every word you said cost a dollar, but you were raised to be polite and never use one word when five will do.

<pause>

I'm hemorrhaging money just to be nice.

- "Expensive by design"

**What doesn't:**
- "Hemorrhaging money" is a bit much
- Needs tighter ending
- The politeness angle overlaps with Politeness Prison bit
- Could be funnier
