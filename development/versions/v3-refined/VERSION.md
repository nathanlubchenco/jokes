## Key Realizations

### From SELF_CRITIQUE.md:

1. **Interesting ≠ Funny**
   - Was keeping material because "philosophically interesting"
   - Real comedians only keep what gets laughs

2. **Economy of Language**
   - 10 words → 5 words hits harder
   - Every word must earn its place

3. **Tags Must Escalate**
   - If tag 3 isn't funnier than tag 2, cut it
   - Diminishing returns kill momentum

4. **One Perfect Example > Three Okay Ones**
   - Specificity over quantity
   - Pick the BEST, commit, move on

5. **Explanation Kills Comedy**
   - Trust the audience
   - Show don't tell
   - Let the joke land

## What Real Comedians Do

```
Week 1: Write 20 minutes
Week 2: Cut to 10 (kill your darlings)
Week 3: Test it, see what works
Week 4: Cut to 7 (only what killed)
Week 5: Rewrite weak spots
Week 6: Test again
Repeat forever
```
